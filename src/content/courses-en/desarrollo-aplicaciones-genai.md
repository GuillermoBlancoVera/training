## Objectives

- Understand the components of a GenAI application beyond chat.
- Apply evaluation, security and observability from the design stage.

## Curriculum

- Model APIs, prompts and structured outputs
- Embeddings, vector databases and RAG
- Agents, tool calling and guardrails
- Evaluation, observability and architecture

## Methodology

Technical workshop with explicit design decisions and integration exercises.
