## Objectives

- Distinguish AI, Machine Learning, Deep Learning and Generative AI.
- Weigh opportunities, limits and risks without oversimplifying.

## Curriculum

- Fundamental AI concepts
- Machine Learning, Deep Learning, LLMs and GenAI
- Use cases, limitations and risks
- Adoption criteria for companies

## Methodology

A clear, participatory session with no technical requirements, based on examples and decision-making questions.
