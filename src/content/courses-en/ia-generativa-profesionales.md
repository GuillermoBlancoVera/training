- Understand the essentials of how language models work.
- Use context, privacy and verification consciously.

## Curriculum

- LLMs, context and hallucinations
- Prompting, RAG and agents
- Security, privacy and governance
- Business use cases

## Methodology

Demos and discussion of concrete scenarios to build practical judgement.
