## Objectives

- Save time on everyday HR tasks without compromising personal data.
- Understand why using AI to select or assess people carries legal and bias risks.
- Prepare the department to lead AI adoption across the rest of the company.

## Curriculum

- Practical uses: job postings, interviews, onboarding, internal communication and engagement surveys
- Candidate and employee data: what must never be entered into public tools
- AI in recruitment and assessment: bias, automated decisions (GDPR) and high-risk systems under the EU AI Act
- Candidates who use AI: how to adapt the recruitment process
- How to assess HR software vendors that include AI
- HR as a driver of change: staff concerns, training and an internal AI use policy

## Methodology

Hands-on workshop built around the department's own cases. Includes an AI use policy template the company can adapt.
