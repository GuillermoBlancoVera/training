## Objectives

- Build and publish a real, simple and fast website, with no monthly platform fees.
- Use AI as an assistant, understanding and reviewing what it generates.
- Know how to update the site and solve basic problems without starting over.

## Curriculum

- Planning: goal, structure, copy and images
- Asking AI for code: just enough HTML and CSS to understand and fix it
- Mobile-friendly design, accessibility and good practices
- Free hosting, a custom domain and HTTPS
- Contact form, basic SEO and link previews on social media
- Legal basics: legal notice, privacy and cookies

## Methodology

An end-to-end workshop: every participant leaves with their website published. AI writes much of the code; the course teaches how to direct it, review the result and maintain it.
