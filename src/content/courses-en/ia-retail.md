## Objectives

- Identify store and head-office tasks where AI genuinely saves time.
- Understand what lies behind recommendations, demand forecasting and dynamic pricing.
- Know the risks of customer-facing AI and how to avoid them.

## Curriculum

- Head office: product listings, campaigns, translations and review analysis
- Stores: onboarding new staff, looking up procedures and organising shifts
- AI the customer does not see: recommendations, demand forecasting and pricing
- Chatbots and customer service: what they can promise and who is liable when they get it wrong
- Customer data, privacy and trust

## Methodology

A hands-on session with examples from the retailer itself, designed for both store and head-office teams.
