## Objectives

- Structure clear, verifiable requests.
- Create reusable patterns for frequent tasks.

## Curriculum

- Context, roles, examples and iteration
- Structured outputs and verification
- Documentation, analysis, emails, code and brainstorming
- Limits: when not to use AI and how to review results

## Methodology

A very practical course. It treats prompting not as magic but as a communication and review skill.
