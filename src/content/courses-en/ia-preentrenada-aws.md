## Objectives

- Know which problems AWS pre-trained AI services solve and when to choose them over generative AI or a custom model.
- Integrate them into applications with security, cost control and measurable quality.

## Curriculum

- Vision: Amazon Rekognition
- Documents: Amazon Textract
- Language: Amazon Comprehend and Amazon Translate
- Speech: Amazon Transcribe and Amazon Polly
- Integration patterns with Lambda, S3 and Step Functions
- Cost, privacy, accuracy and when to move to Amazon Bedrock or SageMaker

## Methodology

Demos and guided exercises with real cases, comparing each service's results with its limitations.
