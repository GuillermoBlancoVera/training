## Objectives

- Understand what Cloud Computing offers and when it makes sense.
- Tell IaaS, PaaS and SaaS apart through practical examples.
- Gain a framework for conversations with technical teams and providers.

## Curriculum

- On-premise versus Cloud
- Service models and shared responsibility
- Regions, availability zones and scalability
- Security, cost and governance
- AWS, Azure and Google Cloud: a comparative view
- Real-world use cases

## Methodology

Visual, accessible explanations connected to product, data and enterprise system scenarios.
