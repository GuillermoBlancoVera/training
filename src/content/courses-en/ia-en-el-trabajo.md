## Objectives

- Understand, without jargon, what generative AI is, what it does well and where it fails.
- Ask for useful results in a structured way and review them with judgement.
- Know what information should never be entered and how to protect customer and company data.

## Curriculum

- AI, Machine Learning and generative AI: what lies behind each term
- How a language model "thinks": context, probabilities and fabrications
- Asking well: context, role, examples, format and iteration
- Cross-functional use cases: writing, summarising, analysing, preparing meetings and documentation
- Privacy, confidentiality and the difference between free and business versions
- Limits: when not to use AI and how to review what it produces

## Methodology

A very hands-on session using the team's own tools. Every participant leaves with a small library of templates for their work and a responsible-use guide.
